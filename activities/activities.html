SceneHub4D

Dynamic 4D Scene Dataset

A multimodal dataset of dynamic indoor environments containing synchronized RGB-D video, camera trajectories, point clouds, textured meshes, and Gaussian splats.

Research areas: Spatial AI, world models, 4D reconstruction, neural rendering, robotics, and XR.

Live Demos

RobotTwin: Human-Robot Collaboration in XR

This demo showcases a mixed reality-based digital twin system for human-robot collaboration in industrial environments. The XR interface enables intuitive visualization of robot tasks, enhancing safety and coordination in factory settings.

XRFab: XR Cleanroom Training Simulator

XRFab simulates cleanroom procedures for semiconductor manufacturing. Trainees interact with wafers and tools using hand tracking on Meta Quest 3, building procedural confidence in a safe, immersive environment.

Interactive XR Assistant for Interventional Radiologists

This demo features a context-aware assistant designed to support interventional radiologists using XR overlays. The system helps visualize anatomical landmarks, procedural steps, and real-time data in a minimally invasive clinical setting.

Outreach Activities

Hosting High School Students via SPLASH Program

As part of the NASA Space Suit Challenge Program under the Artemis mission, we hosted high school students via Northeastern’s SPLASH program. Students were introduced to cutting-edge XR technologies and participated in hands-on activities with state-of-the-art mixed reality headsets like Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest, Microsoft Hololens, etc. This program aimed to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers by providing them with immersive experiences and insights into XR technology.
