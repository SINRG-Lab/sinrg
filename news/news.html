News

August 2026

Two Papers on XR and AI Glasses Accepted to ACM MobiCom 2026 and ACM SenSys 2027

Congratulations to Majd on two paper acceptances on XR and AI glasses! Our MobiCom 2026 paper presents a comprehensive measurement study of the emerging glasses ecosystem, examining their performance, applications, and dependence on smartphones. Our SenSys 2027 paper explores capacitive sensing for low-power eye tracking directly on XR and AI smart glasses.

June 2026

Open4D selected for AWE USA 2026 Builder Stage

Open4D was selected for the Augmented World Expo (AWE) USA 2026 Builder Stage, a highly competitive program featuring 23 selected projects from 132 applicants. The presentation highlighted Open4D's vision for an open ecosystem supporting dynamic 4D geometry for spatial AI, XR, robotics, and autonomous systems.

May 2026

NSF awards Open4D POSE Phase I Grant

SINRG received an NSF Pathways to Enable Open-Source Ecosystems (POSE) Phase I award to establish the Open4D ecosystem. The project supports the development of open standards, interoperable software, and a community-driven platform for dynamic 4D geometry spanning spatial AI, XR, robotics, autonomous systems, and digital twins.

May 2026

SINRG Lab Member - Sungwon In joins Kyung Hee University as Tenure-Track Faculty

Congratulations to Dr. Sungwon In on joining Kyung Hee University as an Assistant Professor. During his time at SINRG as a postdoctoral researcher, Sungwon made significant contributions to Human-AI spatial systems research, helped establish DoD/NSA research collaborations through the LAS program at NCSU, published in premier venues including IEEE VR and IEEE ISMAR, and mentored graduate and undergraduate researchers. We wish him continued success in building his new research group.

April 2026

Mallesh receives NSF CAREER Award

This award builds the systems and networking backbone needed to make spatial intelligence practical, enabling devices like XR and AI glasses to understand and share dynamic 3D environments in real time. It helps move immersive technologies from prototypes to everyday use while supporting new research and education opportunities.

January 2026

NSA/DoD Subcontract Awarded for Human-AI Spatial Intelligence Research

Our team at Northeastern, in collaboration with Virginia Tech, has received a subcontract supported by the National Security Agency through the Laboratory for Analytic Sciences at NC State. The project focuses on human-AI augmented intelligence for embodied spatial coding interfaces. Led by Mallesh Dasari, with Chris North (Virginia Tech) and Sungwon In (Northeastern) as co-PIs, the effort advances next-generation spatial coding and interaction systems.

October 2025

Barath and Mayank show off RoboTwin demo at NVIDIA GTC in D.C.

Representing AI-RAN Alliance, we have demonstrated an edge AI-driven remote human robot collaboration in XR demo at NVIDIA DTC event in D.C. The demo shows off a robot sitting in Boston connected to a 5G gNodeB, operated by users via XR headsets like Quest3 connected via a hotspot of 5G network in D.C.
